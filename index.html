It never touches the game.

ChromaBoost works on the finished picture, after the game has drawn it – like a color filter on your monitor.

No injection

Nothing is loaded into the game. ChromaBoost uses Windows' own color tools and your graphics driver.

Always one key away

Ctrl + Alt + R puts every color back to normal, even in the middle of a match. A crash can't leave your screen colored.

Your data stays home

No servers, no tracking. Your sign-in, presets and settings stay on your PC. Privacy policy
