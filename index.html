ChromaBoost

A Windows app for gamers: stronger, clearer colors on your screen, without touching the game.

  • Saturation up to 300 %, contrast, brightness, temperature and gamma
  • Presets per game, Dark Boost and zoom on a key
  • Your own crosshair, imported from CS2, VALORANT or Crosshair X
  • Sound: volume per app and an equalizer
  • Control it from your phone
