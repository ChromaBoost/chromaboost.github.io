Privacy policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

ChromaBoost is a Windows app that changes your screen's colors, shows a crosshair and controls your sound. It runs entirely on your own computer. There is no ChromaBoost server.

Sign in with Google

ChromaBoost asks you to sign in with your Google account. The sign-in happens in your own web browser on Google's pages. ChromaBoost only asks Google for your name, email address and profile picture (the scopes openid, email and profile).

  • This information is saved only in a file on your own computer (%AppData%\ChromaBoost), to show who is signed in.
  • ChromaBoost gives the access back to Google right after sign-in. It keeps no passwords, tokens or keys.
  • It is never sent to us or to anyone else. We cannot see who uses ChromaBoost.
  • Signing out in ChromaBoost deletes it from your computer. You can also remove ChromaBoost's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

ChromaBoost's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Everything else

  • Settings and presets are stored only on your computer.
  • No tracking: no analytics, no ads, no crash reports and no update checks.
  • Phone control works only inside your own home network while you have the phone window open. Nothing goes over the internet.
  • Sound and background pictures are handled locally on your computer.
